Welcome to Cafe Aurora!

Located in the heart of Liverpool, Cafe Aurora is a cozy, family-run cafe serving hearty breakfasts, fresh lunches, and delicious homemade meals. Whether you're popping in for a coffee and a slice of cake, or settling in for a full meal with friends and family, we offer a warm atmosphere and quality food made with care.

Our menu features a variety of options, from traditional English breakfasts to lighter salads and wraps, as well as children's meals and daily specials. All our dishes are prepared fresh and can be enjoyed in our relaxed seating area or as takeaway.

We pride ourselves on excellent service, fresh ingredients, and a menu that's designed for everyone — whether you're grabbing a quick bite or enjoying a leisurely meal.
