Welcome to Cafe Aurora

Located in the heart of Liverpool, Cafe Aurora offers a warm, friendly environment where you can enjoy delicious, homemade food.

From hearty breakfasts to tasty kids’ meals, salads, pastas, burgers, and more — every dish is freshly prepared with care.

Whether you're stopping by for a coffee, a full meal, or a takeaway, we are here to make your experience special.
