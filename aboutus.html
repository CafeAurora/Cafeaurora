Our Story

Welcome to Cafe Aurora! Located in the heart of Liverpool, we are passionate about serving delicious homemade meals, hearty breakfasts, and family favorites. Whether you're popping in for a quick coffee or enjoying a leisurely lunch, our friendly team is here to make you feel right at home.

Our Promise

We believe in fresh ingredients, quality cooking, and creating a cozy atmosphere where everyone is welcome. From our famous breakfasts to our kids' meals and comforting pastas, we have something for everyone. We also offer collection and delivery to bring Cafe Aurora straight to your doorstep!
