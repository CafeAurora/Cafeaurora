About Us

At Cafe Aurora, we believe in creating a warm and welcoming place where every visit feels like home.

Located in the heart of Liverpool at 118 Hunts Cross Avenue, our family-run cafe offers fresh breakfasts, delicious lunches, homemade treats, and friendly smiles daily.

All our food is prepared with love, using high-quality, locally sourced ingredients. Whether you're enjoying a hearty breakfast, a light salad, or a cozy cup of coffee with cake, Cafe Aurora is the perfect spot to relax and recharge.

We also offer takeaway, collection, and delivery options, making it easy for you to enjoy our food wherever you are.

We can't wait to welcome you!
